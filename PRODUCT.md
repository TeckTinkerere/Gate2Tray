# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is a solo cabin-commerce operator for a small charter airline. They need to understand which flights require attention, approve bounded commercial actions, manage inventory risk and exceptions, and remain accountable for what the operations agent executes. Cabin crew need a glanceable, one- or two-tap fulfillment queue in constrained cabin conditions. Passengers need a focused mobile ordering and exception-resolution flow that reassures them their meal remains reserved.

## Product Purpose

Gate2Tray OS is a reservation-and-fulfillment orchestration layer for inventory already loaded onto a flight. It forecasts demand, recommends policy-bounded discounts, publishes flight-specific ordering, coordinates crew fulfillment, and resolves unavailable meals through passenger-approved substitutions or simulated refunds. The product proves that one operator can set policy and exercise judgment while an agent performs repetitive execution with a visible audit trail.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and quietly capable. The product should reduce operational anxiety, explain recommendations in plain language, and demonstrate intelligence through useful completed workflows instead of theatrical AI decoration. The primary reference is Stripe Dashboard for hierarchy, transaction states, auditability, and restrained confidence. Linear informs the speed and focus of the operator queue; Apple Wallet and modern mobile checkout patterns inform the passenger experience. The internal direction is “Stripe operational clarity with airline hospitality.”

## Anti-references

Avoid generic blue-gradient airline SaaS, dark cockpit dashboards, sci-fi AI interfaces, glassmorphism, dense enterprise tables on every screen, oversized product heroes, chat-first operations, excessive animation, tiny chips, icon-only controls, color-only status, decorative route graphics, and fake precision. It should not resemble Palantir, an avionics panel, a generic food-delivery application, or an airline booking site with an AI feature added afterward.

## Design Principles

- Make the operating sequence obvious: observe, recommend, approve, execute, log.
- Put attention first: surface pending approvals, inventory risk, active exceptions, revenue impact, and the agent trail in that order.
- Explain every consequential recommendation through observed evidence, checked policy, intended action, and expected result.
- Reserve urgency for genuine exceptions; routine operations should feel calm and controlled.
- Optimize each surface for its context: accountable control for operators, arm’s-length glanceability for crew, and reassurance for passengers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with minimum 44×44 px touch targets, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard navigation, text labels alongside important icons, status communicated through text, shape, and color, reduced-motion support, and no essential hover-only content. Include clear loading, empty, offline, and error states; use plain language instead of airline abbreviations; provide large one-handed crew controls; ensure contrast works in bright gate areas and dim cabins; require persistent confirmation for irreversible actions and provide undo where operationally safe.
